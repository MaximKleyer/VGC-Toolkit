# Mega Golisopod Hard Trick Room (Regulation M-C)

Built with Wolfe's playbook procedure (section 2.12) and the toolkit's calcs.
Paste: `golisopod_hard_trick_room.txt` (import it in the Team Builder or the Battle tab).

## The idea
- **Starting point:** Mega Golisopod (Bug/Steel, 40 Speed, 150 Atk, 175 Def). Under Trick Room
  it moves first and its Bug STAB (First Impression 100 BP, +2 priority) hits the meta's own
  Trick Room setters (Farigiraf, Sinistcha) and Grass types for double; Iron Head answers the
  Fairy Megas (Mega Floette, Sylveon); Close Combat answers Kingambit; Rock Slide answers
  Mega Charizard Y once the room is up. Its holes are Fire (4x) and Ground (2x), and the
  Mega's ability is still provisional (Battle Armor here; the engine ships Emergency Exit).
- **Speed control:** Trick Room from two bulky setters (Farigiraf, Sinistcha). Outside the room the
  team leans on priority (First Impression, Aqua Jet) plus Icy Wind, and on bulk.
- **Why each partner is here**
  - Farigiraf: setter #1, Armor Tail blocks Fake Out, Sucker Punch and Prankster Encore aimed at
    your side, Helping Hand, Psychic 4x on Sneasler.
  - Sinistcha: setter #2 and Rage Powder redirection; Heatproof and Grass typing resist both of
    Golisopod's weaknesses; Colbur Berry for Kingambit's Kowtow Cleave.
  - Tyranitar: Sand Stream cancels Mega Charizard Y's sun and halves Solar Beam; Rock Slide
    spread (4x on Charizard), Crunch for Basculegion, Low Kick 4x on Kingambit; Fire resist.
  - Primarina: special spread damage (Liquid Voice turns Hyper Voice into Water), Moonblast for
    Garchomp, Icy Wind as backup speed control (4x on Garchomp); resists Fire.
  - Azumarill: Huge Power priority (Aqua Jet) for the out-of-room mode, Play Rough 2x on Garchomp,
    Liquidation 2x on Incineroar and 4x on Hisuian Arcanine; resists Fire and Dark.

## Game plan
1. Lead the setter + support that the opposing lead least wants to see (Farigiraf into Fake Out
   leads; Sinistcha into physical leads). Golisopod and the second sweeper come in once the room
   is up, with Rage Powder covering the switch.
2. Inside the room: First Impression / Close Combat / Rock Slide from Golisopod, Rock Slide from
   Tyranitar, Hyper Voice from Primarina. Aim to decide the game in the first room (Wolfe: "the
   game is usually decided by the damage dealt in the first Trick Room").
3. Mega Charizard Y: never leave Golisopod in versus sun; bring Tyranitar (sand) and Azumarill.
   Kingambit: Close Combat / Low Kick / Hyper Voice, and keep Farigiraf on the field so Sucker
   Punch is blocked. Garchomp: Icy Wind or Play Rough before it Earthquakes; it is the main
   threat to Golisopod and Tyranitar outside the room.

## Known trade-offs (from the playbook check)
- Grass weakness is stacked on Tyranitar, Primarina and Azumarill. Hatterene in place of Primarina
  removes the stack and adds a third setter with Magic Bounce (Taunt immunity), but loses the
  Fire resist, Icy Wind and the Water spread that punishes this Fire-heavy ladder.
- No Rock resistance; only Sinistcha resists Ground. Garchomp's Earthquake is the one hit the
  team has to play around rather than absorb.
- Tyranitar's best STAB is Crunch (80 BP): fine for its role (Rock Slide spread + sand).
